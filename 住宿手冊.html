Guest House Manual

一、安全提示

Safety Information

1. 消防安全提示 / Fire Safety

为守护您的安全，屋内已配备灭火器（位于廚房下方）。
民宿室內外嚴禁吸烟及电子烟、若造成物品損害,照价賠償。馬來西亞法令規定公共空間禁煙,違反者處予高額罰款。
若遇火災，沿逃生路线有序撤离，紧急情况可拨打紧急电话或999。
温馨提醒：房间内所有插座均为额定电压220V，请勿插入非适配电器，避免短路引发安全隐患。

For your safety, a fire extinguisher is provided (located under the kitchen area). Smoking and e-cigarettes are strictly prohibited indoors and outdoors. Any damage caused will be charged accordingly. Malaysian law prohibits smoking in public areas, and violations may result in heavy fines. In case of fire, please evacuate calmly following the escape route. For emergencies, dial 999.
Kindly note: All sockets are rated at 220V. Please do not use incompatible electrical devices to avoid short circuits or hazards.

2. 防盗安全提示 / Security

离开房间时请务必关好房门，贵重物品建议妥当存放，或随身携带。若发现房门锁具异常、陌生人敲门，请勿轻易开门并立即联系屋主。

Please ensure the door is locked when leaving the room. Keep your valuables secure or carry them with you. If you notice any issues with the door lock or encounter strangers knocking, do not open the door and contact the host immediately.

3. 厨房安全提示 / Kitchen Safety

使用厨房时，灶台周边避免摆放易燃物品，廚房使用时，人不离灶。
闻到燃气异味，快关电器、开窗通风，勿动电器以免誤觸。
电水壶加水请勿超过最高刻度，煮沸后及时断电，防止干烧损坏或引发危险。

Do not place flammable items near the stove. Never leave cooking unattended. If you smell gas, turn off the gas supply, open windows for ventilation, and do not operate any electrical appliances. Do not exceed the maximum water level when using the electric kettle. Switch it off promptly after boiling.

4. 卫生间安全提示 / Bathroom Safety

使用浴室熱水器等大功率电电器，离开时请隨手关闭电源。
若发现水管漏水、热水器异常（如异响、漏水），请立即关闭阀门并联系屋主。

Please switch off power when not using high-power appliances such as the water heater. If you notice water leakage or heater malfunction (noise, leaks), turn off the valve immediately and contact the host.

二、住宿行为提示

House Rules

1. 噪音与作息 / Noise & Quiet Hours

为给您及其他住客营造舒适环境，建议每日 22:00 后主动降低室内音量，轻声交谈。避免高跟鞋频繁走动、搬运重物等声响，共同守护休息时光。

To ensure a comfortable stay, please keep noise levels low after 10:00 PM. Avoid loud footsteps and moving heavy objects that may cause disturbance.

2. 物品使用与爱护 / Use of Property

房间家具均为精心挑选，请勿站立或挪动重物撞击，损坏需按成本价赔偿。电器已调试至最佳状态，遇问题请联系屋主。床品一客一换，若沾染顽固污渍需额外支付清洗费，建议在桌前进食。

Please do not stand on or roughly move furniture. Damage will be charged at replacement cost. Contact host for appliance help. Bedding is changed per guest; tough stains incur cleaning charges. Please eat at the dining table.

3. 明确责任 / Responsibility

禁止在公共區域,樓梯間,閣樓上奔跑嬉戲,若发生意外受傷或設施損壞,由家長或監護人承擔責任。

Running/playing in public areas is prohibited. Injuries/damages are the responsibility of the guest/guardian.

4. 宠物 / Pets Policy

为保障所有住客的健康，本民宿禁止携带宠物入住, 感谢您的配合。

Pets are not allowed in the property. Thank you for your understanding.

5. 遵守規定 / Compliance

嚴禁吸菸、酗酒、轟趴、嗑藥，亦不可擅自烹飪或烤肉。

Smoking, excessive drinking, parties, drugs, and unauthorized cooking or barbecuing are strictly prohibited.

民宿免责 Disclaimer: 透過此公告方式,試圖將因客人行為不當所產生的意外風險降至最低 / Guidelines are to minimize risks caused by improper behavior.

三、温馨提示

Friendly Reminders

1. 生活便利提示 / Daily Convenience

卫生间：抽屉内备有洗发精及沐浴乳，洗澡请注意脚下防滑。
厨房：免费使用，餐具已消毒。使用后请清洗归位，造成髒亂將收取清潔費；冰箱可存放食材，退房前请清理个人物品。

Bathroom: Disposable shampoo/body wash provided. Careful of slippery floors.
Kitchen: Free to use. Clean and return utensils after use; excessive mess incurs fees. Remove personal items from fridge before check-out.

  • 步行5分钟至各种市场

3-min walk to E-MART (24h), 5-min to markets. Convenience store 100m downstairs right. Self-laundry nearby.

3. 特殊天气 / Weather

雨天，樓梯处备有雨伞（免费借用，退房归还）。出门前请关好门窗；走廊地面湿滑，请慢步。

Umbrellas available near staircase (return at check-out). Close windows/doors before leaving. Walk carefully on wet floors.
